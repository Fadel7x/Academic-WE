Essay Vocabulary Blueprint

Quick cheat-sheet to use during university essay brainstorming

1. Essay Introduction & Thesis Framing

Use these verbs and phrases to establish the context and state your main line of argument.

Delineate: To outline or describe precisely.
"This essay delineates the key socio-economic factors..."
Premise: An underlying assumption.
"Based on the premise that global temperatures rise..."
Imperative: Crucial or vital necessity.
"It is imperative to examine the policy implications..."
Elucidate: To make clear / explain.
"The objective is to elucidate the mechanisms behind..."

2. Supporting Claims & Evidence

Phrases to present data, research findings, and reinforce your arguments.

Substantiate: To prove or support with evidence.
"Empirical data substantiates this hypothesis..."
Corroborate: To confirm or strengthen a finding.
"Recent studies corroborate earlier findings..."
Facilitate: To make an action or process easier.
"Targeted investments facilitate economic growth..."
Exemplify: To serve as a typical example.
"This case study exemplifies the broader trend..."

3. Counterarguments & Transitions

Introduce opposing perspectives and transition smoothly between points.

Conversely: In contrast / on the other hand.
"Conversely, critics argue that the costs outweigh..."
Notwithstanding: In spite of / despite.
"Notwithstanding these challenges, progress persists..."
Paradoxically: In a contradictory manner.
"Paradoxically, increased security led to anxiety..."
Advocate: To publicly recommend or support.
"Scholars advocate for immediate institutional reform..."
